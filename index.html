OUR PURPOSE

Our mission.
Our action.

The mission of Social Work Students in Action (SWSA) is to empower and equip students to become compassionate leaders, dedicated advocates, and agents of positive social change. Through community service, professional development, leadership opportunities, and meaningful connections, SWSA strives to put social work values into action while making a lasting difference in the lives of individuals, families, and communities.
